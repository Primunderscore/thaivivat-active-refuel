# Active Refuel: online demo

Competition prototype for Thaivivat Innovation Camp 5 (not medical advice).
Open: https://primunderscore.github.io/thaivivat-active-refuel/

Real Lotus's products, photos and prices (lotuss.com, fetched 25 Nov 2026; the 32 products the engine recommends were
re-read on 27 Nov 2026 at Lotus's Hyper Bang Kapi with the same prices). Deal terms: revenue model v6 (Lotus's only,
2% fee to the TVH subsidiary, bundle discount paid by Lotus's, TVI Well Point for Active Health customers).
Nutrients from each product's label or the
Thai Department of Health food composition table; real watch days from Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23344939 (CC BY 4.0).
The recommendations were computed in advance by the team's Python prototype for every demo day and every 30 minutes.
Product photos belong to Lotus's and the brands.
